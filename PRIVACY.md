# Privacy Policy — X Tweet Assistant (X 推文助手)

**Effective date:** 2026-11-27
**Extension:** X Tweet Assistant (X 推文半自动运营插件), version 1.6.0
**Developer:** zxj0051-alt (https://github.com/zxj0051-alt/x-tweet-assistant)

## English

### Data we collect
**We collect nothing.** The extension does not collect, transmit, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data, browsing history, or usage analytics.

### What stays on your device
- **Your AI API keys** (GLM, SiliconFlow, Gemini, Claude) are stored only in your own browser via `chrome.storage.sync`. They are sent only to the AI provider you selected, for the sole purpose of generating draft text. They are never sent to the developer or any third party.
- **Your settings** (provider, language, style, hot words) are stored only in your own browser via `chrome.storage.sync`.

### What the extension reads
- The extension reads the X (Twitter) page you are currently viewing, only to extract the text and images of a tweet you clicked and the trending topics visible on the page, so it can generate reply drafts and post copy for you.
- It does not read your private messages, your account credentials, or pages other than x.com.

### What the extension does NOT do
- It does not auto-post, auto-reply, like, follow, retweet, or perform any action on X. All posting is done manually by you.
- It does not track you, does not run analytics, and does not show ads.

Questions: https://github.com/zxj0051-alt/x-tweet-assistant/issues or https://x.com/zj19890730

## 中文

### 我们收集的数据
**我们不收集任何数据。** 本插件不收集、不传输、不在任何服务器上存储、不分享、不出售任何个人数据、浏览记录或使用统计。

### 只保存在你设备上的内容
- **你的 AI API Key**（智谱 GLM、硅基流动、Gemini、Claude）只保存在你自己的浏览器里（`chrome.storage.sync`），仅发送给你自己选定的 AI 提供商，用于生成文案，绝不会发给开发者或任何第三方。
- **你的设置**（提供商、语言、风格、热词）只保存在你自己的浏览器里（`chrome.storage.sync`）。

### 插件读取的内容
- 插件只读取你当前正在看的 X（Twitter）页面，用于提取你点开的推文文字/图片和页面上可见的热搜话题，以便为你生成回复草稿和发帖文案。
- 不读取你的私信、账号密码，也不读取 x.com 以外的页面。

### 插件不做的事
- 不自动发帖、不自动回复、不点赞、不关注、不转发，所有发布都由你手动完成。
- 不追踪你、不做数据统计、不展示广告。
